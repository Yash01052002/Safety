## Who we are
Suraksha ("we") provides a personal-safety app that lets you alert trusted
contacts and share your live location in an emergency.

## What we collect
- **Account data:** your name and phone number.
- **Trusted contacts:** names and phone numbers you add as guardians.
- **Location data:** collected **only while an SOS or a live-share session is
  active**. We do not track your location in the background at any other time.
- **Emergency media (optional):** short audio and/or a photo you choose to
  capture during an SOS.
- **Device token:** a push-notification token so guardians and you can receive
  alerts.
- **Diagnostics:** crash and performance data (Firebase Crashlytics/Analytics).

## How we use it
- To deliver your SOS and live location to the guardians **you** choose.
- To notify you when a guardian responds.
- To keep the service reliable (diagnostics).

We do **not** sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Sharing
- Location and media are shared only with your chosen guardians, via
  **time-limited links that expire automatically**.
- We use service providers — Google Firebase (auth, database, storage, push)
  and a messaging provider (e.g. Twilio) for SMS — who process data on our
  behalf under their terms.

## Retention
- Resolved SOS events and their media are deleted after **90 days**.
- Public tracking links expire within **24 hours** (sooner once you mark
  yourself safe).
- You can delete your account and **all** data at any time from
  **Privacy & data → Delete my account & data**.
