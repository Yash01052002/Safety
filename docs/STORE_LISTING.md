## Short description (Play, 80 chars)
Shake to send an SOS and share your live location with people you trust.

## Full description
Suraksha helps you get help fast. Shake your phone or hold the SOS button to
instantly alert your trusted contacts with your live location — even with the
screen locked. Share your live location for a trip, and let a guardian follow
you until you arrive safely.

**Features**
- 🚨 Shake-to-alert SOS (tunable sensitivity, stealth mode)
- 📍 Live location sharing with a duration you choose
- 👪 Trusted contacts with escalation priority
- 🔔 Guardians get a push or an SMS with a live map link — no app required
- 🗣️ Guardian responses ("I'm on my way" / "Call police")
- 🎙️ Optional audio evidence on SOS
- 🔦 Optional siren + flashlight strobe
- ⌚ iOS Action Button / Back Tap / Siri Shortcut support

Your privacy comes first: location is shared only during an active alert or
share, through links that expire automatically. Delete your data anytime.
